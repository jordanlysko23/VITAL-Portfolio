VITAL Portfolio

Thylacine Biotherapeutics is an early-stage biotechnology company developing a novel, proprietary, and adaptable lipopeptide antiviral platform that targets endemic viruses and those that may cause future outbreaks. These inhalable therapeutics treat illness, prevent infection, and limit transmission.

Macrotope, Inc. is a biotech startup that embraces an innovative antigen-centric approach to vaccine development and therapeutic antibody discovery. Their patented MAV technology leverages antigen conformational dynamics to amplify immune responses and enhance epitope coverage, maximizing the likelihood of identifying antibodies with desired properties.

Tenza converts probiotic microbes into living factories for targeted delivery of supplements, therapeutics, and vaccines

New Platelet Company develops manufactured universal platelets designed to expand access to lifesaving blood components. Its approach could strengthen hospital surge capacity, reduce shortages, and support emergency, disaster, and routine healthcare needs.

Cutanos harnesses the power of immune cell-specific drug delivery to develop next-generation therapies for infectious diseases and cancer.

Boost Biopharma develops protein-based vaccines and monoclonal antibodies through its Rapid Antigen Deployment (RAD) platform, engineering pathogen-specific antigens at speeds comparable to mRNA technology. The platform is designed to address issues of immune imprinting that can inhibit vaccine development, producing candidates that are safe, scalable, low-cost, and stable. Its lead SARS-CoV-2 candidate, backed by CEPI, NIH/NIAID, and BARDA, is now in Phase 1 trials.

Avocet Bio is on a mission to develop transformative solutions for global health challenges, with a current focus on protecting high-risk populations from respiratory viral infections. Backed by a dynamic team of innovators, AvocetBio combines cutting-edge technology with a deep commitment to improving patient outcomes and advancing public health.

Grant Knappe is an innovator at the Bathe BioNanoLab working on harnessing nucleic acid nanotechnology to develop more potent and durable vaccines and active immunotherapies.

The Yin Lab at University of Texas at Austin is an immunoengineering lab that develops next-generation nanoparticle adjuvants to enable more effective vaccines against influenza, coronaviruses, and filoviruses.

Epigenter develops a new type of therapeutic protein designed to epigenetically unlock the body’s silenced potential, creating new approaches to treating chronic and intractable diseases with high unmet need.

Fletcher Biosciences develops precision immunotherapies that selectively target disease-associated immune cells. The company's approach is designed to enable highly specific treatments with improved safety profiles.

Cryptyx Bioscience harnesses an innovative and proprietary drug discovery platform to unlock the potential of cryptic metabolites and address today's most pressing and unmet health challenges.

InflammaSense builds the VITALx-patch, a wearable platform that records Autonomic Neurography, or ANG, as a new vital sign. By transmitting disease severity to clinicians with millisecond precision, the technology aims to improve therapeutic efficacy.

Safi Biotherapeutics Inc. is developing a cost-efficient manufactured human red blood cell (mRBC) product at scale to address military and civilian blood shortages, including freeze dried and glycerol-free frozen mRBC formulations for pre-hospital use and military and government stockpiling.

Vaayu Therapeutics is a preclinical-stage biotech company developing novel small-molecule kinase inhibitors to treat severe influenza and related acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS). Founded by researchers from institutions including Tufts University and Fox Chase Cancer Center, the company focuses on blocking cell death to prevent fatal lung damage.

Nitro Biosciences is a company developing a next-generation antigen engineering platform

AuraVax Therapeutics is commercializing its intranasal platform termed “NanoSTING” to treat and prevent respiratory infections independent of the pathogen. NanoSTING technology safely and sustainably activates the STING pathway to restore and boost innate immunity. NanoSTING-001 is a broad-acting intranasal host-directed-therapeutic to treat infections from respiratory pathogens, and NanoSTING-002 is a mucosal adjuvant to improve immune responses to vaccines.

Narval is developing Synthetic Antibody Mimetic Proteins (AMPs), a next-generation alternative to conventional antibodies, delivered through mucosal or skin tissues to treat diseases of the eyes, lungs, and skin. Its platform aims to make antibody therapeutics safer, more accessible, and less costly for rapid-response medical countermeasures.

Pavi Health reimagines precision medicine for severe infections and sepsis, providing critical information to improve clinical outcomes and reduce costs.

Cell BioEngines develops off-the-shelf allogeneic cell therapies by chemically reprogramming stem cell fate and function to treat serious and currently incurable diseases.

Sagitta Biotech develops innovative vaccines featuring high immunogenicity, short development time, and constant manufacturing process in order to tackle unmet needs in the developed and developing worlds.

Cyrus Biotechnology is a computationally-driven protein engineering company that transforms naturally occurring proteins into superior therapeutics using advanced modeling and deep mutagenesis. Its lead programs target severe autoimmune disease and Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS) through engineered biologics.

Immunyx Pharma is developing novel immune modulating therapies by targeting the toxic activity of the neutrophil in infectious and inflammatory disease. Their first-in-class orally available drug, IMYX-3 has been shown effective in safe in animal models and is positioned to reach clinical evaluation in the coming year.

Spectre Bio develops genome-inactivated bacterial-like particles, or BLiPs, that train mucosal innate immunity against incoming pathogens, which can be deployable anytime and anywhere.

Montic One Lab at Louisiana State University transforms immunosuppression research into next-generation immunotherapeutics.
